Lower import duty from Cambodia

Cambodia is one of the UN's Least Developed Countries, so goods from our Kampong Speu factory get trade preferences in six major markets.

European Union

GSP preference: duty relief on imports into EU countries since January 2011.

United States

Most Favoured Nation status, with preferential terms for textiles and garments.

Japan

ASEAN–Japan agreement: exempt from the usual 10–12% import tax, and quota-free.

Canada

Quota-free and duty-free under Canada's preferential tariff for LDC countries.

South Korea

Quota-free and duty-free import with minimum restrictions.

Australia

Tax-free status in line with other developed-market preferences.

Exact duty depends on the product and its materials. Tell us your market and we will confirm the best factory for your order.
