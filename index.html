Lower import duty when you ship from Cambodia

Cambodia is one of the UN's Least Developed Countries, so goods from our Kampong Speu factory get trade preferences in major markets.

European Union

GSP preference: duty relief on imports into EU countries since January 2011.

United States

Most Favoured Nation status, with preferential terms for textiles and garments.

Japan

Canada

South Korea

Quota-free and duty-free import with minimum restrictions.

Australia

Tax-free status in line with other developed-market preferences.
